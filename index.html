Our Story

Born In A Clawfoot Tub

“It started as a hobby. It became the work.”

In 2017, Capt. Tie Dye hatched on the shores of Keystone Lake, Oklahoma. Like a love child of Frosty the Snowman and Captain Underpants, CTD popped in with his top hat and pipe, his cape, and the long johns of many colors.

When family medical issues arose, what started as a hobby began to take center stage. Pot ash, twine, boiling water and ice combined in the clawfoot tub of a converted shipping container to meet financial needs. CTD takes pride in his humble beginnings and continues to create each one-of-a-kind piece with his own hands.
