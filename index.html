Our Story

Born In A Clawfoot Tub

“It started as a hobby. It became the work.”

In 2017, Capt. Tie Dye hatched on the shores of Keystone Lake, Oklahoma. When family medical issues arose, what started as a hobby began to take center stage. Pot ash, twine, boiling water and ice combined in the clawfoot tub of a converted shipping container to meet financial needs.

Dyed For America takes pride in these humble beginnings and continues to create each one-of-a-kind piece with the same passion and hands-on approach.
