Our Story

Born In A Clawfoot Tub

“It started as a hobby. It became the work.”

In 2017, Capt. Tie Dye hatched, fully grown, on the sandy shores of Keystone Lake, Oklahoma. Like a love child of Frosty the Snowman and Captain Underpants, CTD popped in with his top hat and pipe, his cape, and the long johns of many colors. With his trusty sidekicks, Rascal and Grenade, CTD set up camp in the festival scene, accompanied by an ever-changing array of scallywags and maidens of dubious virtue.

When family medical issues arose, what started as a hobby and creative outlet began to take center stage. Pot ash, twine, boiling water and ice combined in the clawfoot tub of a converted shipping container to meet financial needs. Basic spirals and splotches gave way to fractals, bleach, and reverse mandalas.

A small but dedicated following from the mid-west continued growing across the US and Canada, eventually hopping the pond as far as New Zealand. No matter how far his reputation goes, CTD takes pride in his humble beginnings and continues to create each one-of-a-kind piece with his own hands.
