Our Story

Born In A Clawfoot Tub

“It started as a hobby. It became the work.”

In 2017, Capt. Tie Dye hatched on the shores of Keystone Lake, Oklahoma. When family medical issues arose, what started as a hobby began to take center stage, combining art and necessity in a converted shipping container.
